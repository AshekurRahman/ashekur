THE PROBLEM

What a proper audit finds that a glance doesn’t

Most sites look fine at a glance. The problems that actually cap rankings only show up once someone crawls the site and reads the data, not the homepage.

WHAT USUALLY GETS MISSED

  • Pages that look fine to a visitor but were never actually indexed by Google
  • Redirect chains and orphaned pages, invisible without an actual crawl
  • A Core Web Vitals score quietly capping rankings, never measured because nobody looked
  • Duplicate or near-identical pages splitting authority that should be going to one URL
  • Titles, headings and structured data applied inconsistently across the site
  • No baseline at all, so there's no honest way to know later whether anything worked

PROCESS

How the audit runs

  1. Crawl and baseline

    The site is crawled and Search Console and Core Web Vitals data pulled, so the report is grounded in what's actually happening, not a guess.

  2. Check indexation and crawlability

    What's blocked, orphaned, duplicated or quietly deindexed — usually the single largest and cheapest finding on the report.

  3. Audit on-page and structure

    Titles, headings, structured data and internal linking checked across the site, including where pages compete with each other.

  4. Prioritize by impact

    A ranked shortlist, not a 200-row spreadsheet — indexation and Core Web Vitals findings come before meta description polish, in the order they actually affect results.

  5. Deliver the written report

    Every finding, ranked and explained in plain language, ready to hand to any developer — including me, if you decide to book the fix work.

FAQ

Questions I get asked

What people ask before booking, mostly about how this differs from the full SEO service and what's actually included.

How is this different from your full SEO service?

This is diagnosis only — a written report, fixed price, no ongoing commitment. WordPress SEO is the full engagement: the same audit as its first phase, then the fix work itself, then optional monthly monitoring. Start here if you want the diagnosis first and are not ready to commit to more, or already have someone to execute it.

Is the fix work included?

No. You get a written report ranked by impact, not the implementation. Some clients hand the report to their existing developer, some come back to book the fix work separately, and either is a fine outcome — there's no pressure either way.

How long does the audit take?

Most audits are delivered within a week of getting site and Search Console access. Site size and how much history is available in Search Console are the main factors that change it.

Do you guarantee this will improve my rankings?

No one honestly can, and an audit especially can't — it identifies what's wrong, it doesn't fix it. What you get is an accurate, ranked picture of what's actually holding the site back, which is the necessary first step regardless of who does the fix work afterward.

Is this only for WordPress sites?

No. The audit methodology applies to any platform — WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, a custom build. WordPress is simply where I'm fastest, because I know where its particular problems tend to hide.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Most of my clients are outside Bangladesh — the UK, Germany and the US primarily. I am based in Bangladesh and work across European and North American time zones without difficulty, with a same-day reply to anything urgent.

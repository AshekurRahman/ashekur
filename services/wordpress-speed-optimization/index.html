SIGNS YOU NEED THIS

How a slow WordPress site usually shows up

Speed problems rarely have one cause. These are the patterns I see most when a WordPress site is genuinely slow, not just slow in one test.

PageSpeed or Lighthouse score in the red

A low score is a symptom, not the diagnosis. I look at what is actually blocking render, not just the number, before touching anything.

Core Web Vitals failing in Search Console

Google is measuring real visitors, not a lab test, which means the fix has to hold under real network conditions and real devices, not just a synthetic test run.

Fast on desktop, slow on mobile

Most traffic and most of Google’s ranking signal is mobile. A site that looks fine on a fibre connection at a desk can fail badly on a phone on 4G.

Every plugin you add makes it slower

Page builders, form plugins and analytics scripts stack up over time. I audit what is actually earning its place on the page and remove or replace what is not.

PROCESS

How a speed optimization runs

  1. Baseline audit

    Lighthouse, PageSpeed Insights and Search Console data get pulled together first, so we are optimizing against your actual numbers, not a guess.

  2. Find what is actually slow

    Images, unoptimized plugins, render-blocking scripts and slow queries are identified individually rather than treated as one vague problem.

  3. Fix in priority order

    Changes that move the score the most happen first, tested on staging so nothing breaks on the way to being fast.

  4. Deploy and re-measure

    Pushed live, then re-tested under the same conditions as the baseline, so the improvement is measured, not assumed.

  5. Hand over what to watch

    A short report on what changed, what to avoid adding back, and what would slow it down again if ignored.

FAQ

Questions I get asked

What people ask before optimizing a slow WordPress site.

What is a realistic PageSpeed score for a WordPress site?

It depends on the site, but a well-optimized WordPress site with images and content can reasonably reach the high 80s to high 90s on mobile. I will tell you honestly what is achievable for your specific setup rather than promising 100 across the board.

Will you need access to my hosting?

For a full optimization, yes, since caching and server-level configuration matter as much as the WordPress side. If hosting access is not possible, I can still do the on-site work and tell you what hosting is holding back.

Do you guarantee a specific score?

No, and I would not trust anyone who does, since scores vary by test conditions. What I do guarantee is a measurable improvement against your baseline, with the report to show it.

Can an Elementor or Divi site actually be fast?

Yes, with the right configuration, though it takes more careful work than a lightweight custom theme. I have optimized both, and will tell you upfront if the builder itself is the ceiling on how fast the site can get.

How long does optimization take?

A typical site takes a few days from audit to final report. Sites with deeper database or hosting issues can take longer, and I will scope that honestly after the initial audit. See current starting prices for a reference point.

Is this a one-time fix or does it need to be ongoing?

The initial fix is one-time, but performance drifts as plugins update and content gets added. Some clients bring me back periodically or add it to an ongoing maintenance arrangement.

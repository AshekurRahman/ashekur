WHAT MOVES THE PRICE

What affects the final price.

The same handful of factors move cost regardless of platform. See what actually drives website cost for the full breakdown, or what a small business site actually needs if you are budgeting for one specifically.

  • Design complexity — a fully custom build vs. adapting an existing theme or template
  • Content volume and structure — how many pages and content types
  • Integrations — CRM, booking systems, payment processors, inventory feeds
  • E-commerce complexity — catalog size, variants, subscriptions
  • Performance and SEO work — a real line item, not automatic on a new build
  • Ongoing maintenance needs — who runs the site day to day after launch

HOW IT WORKS

Fixed quote, after scoping — not before.

A fixed price on an undefined scope is a fixed price on a guess. This is what happens before you get a number.

  1. Discovery and a fixed quote

    What the site has to achieve, who it is for, what is failing now. You get a written scope and a fixed price before anything starts.

    Page architecture, URLs, heading hierarchy and keyword targets first — part of the same scoped, fixed-price engagement.

  3. Build, then performance and SEO

  4. Launch and handover

    Redirect mapping so existing rankings survive, analytics setup, and a recorded walkthrough so your team can run it.

FAQ

Questions about pricing.

Why is there a range or “starting from” price instead of one number?

Because there is no single price that fairly describes a website. Cost depends on scope, design complexity, integrations, content volume and technical complexity — the starting prices above are a reference point, not a quote for your specific project.

How do you determine the final price?

Through discovery: what the site has to achieve, who it is for, and what is failing now if you already have one. You get a written scope and a fixed price before anything starts — not an estimate that changes once work is underway.

What if my project doesn’t fit these categories?

Get in touch and describe what you are building. Most real projects combine more than one of these anyway — a WordPress site with custom frontend components, or a Webflow build that needs technical SEO work alongside it.

Do you offer any subscription or retainer pricing?

One: WordPress Maintenance, a month-to-month plan covering updates, backups, monitoring and a set amount of small changes, starting at $79/month. Everything else on this page is one-time, project-priced work.

Is a fix or diagnostic job priced differently from a new build?

Yes. A build has a defined scope from the start, so it can carry a starting price. A fix, migration or clean-up depends on what’s actually wrong, which isn’t known until it’s been looked at — so those are quoted after a short look rather than estimated blind.

What’s the difference between a fixed quote and an estimate?

An estimate can change once work is underway. A fixed quote is a written price against a written scope, agreed before anything starts — if the scope changes later, that’s a separate conversation, not a surprise on the invoice.

Do you require a deposit before starting?

Payment terms are agreed as part of the fixed quote before work starts, alongside the scope and timeline — ask when you get in touch and it’ll be laid out plainly.

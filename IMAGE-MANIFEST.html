Naming matters. The filename in each row is what the markup will expect — keep it exactly, lowercase, hyphenated, .jpeg. If your generator only exports PNG, save the PNG under the same base name and I’ll convert it.

Size. 1280×720 is the target. Anything larger at a clean 16:9 (1536×864, 1920×1080) is fine — I’ll downscale. Avoid 1:1 or 4:3 crops; the layout reserves a 16:9 box.

No two alike. The 44 prompts draw on 10 compositions and 6 lighting schemes, and every image gets a pair no other image has, plus its own focal motif. Confusable groups — the three blank-screen images, the three spinners, the three gauges — are forced onto different compositions from each other. If your generator still returns two similar frames, re-roll the one whose composition code is less visible in the result.

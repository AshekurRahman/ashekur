ABOUT

WordPress depth,

I’m a solo freelancer who’s been building WordPress websites since 2015 — custom themes, WooCommerce storefronts, and the unglamorous speed, structure and technical SEO work that decides whether a site actually gets found.

What separates my WordPress work is the engineering behind it: four years leading React front ends at Peymynt and a year on Nuxt.js at Sumashtech.

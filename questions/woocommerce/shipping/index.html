Flat Rate vs. Free Shipping: What's the Difference?

Flat Rate charges a fixed (or calculated) cost every time; Free Shipping charges nothing, optionally requiring a coupon or minimum order amount.

What Happens If an Address Matches Multiple Shipping Zones?

Only the first matching zone, in top-to-bottom list order, is ever used — WooCommerce doesn't combine or choose between multiple matching zones.

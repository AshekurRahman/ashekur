What's the Difference Between Manual and Automatic Renewal in WooCommerce Subscriptions?

SHORT ANSWER

Automatic renewal charges a stored, tokenized payment method (via a gateway like Stripe or PayPal) on the subscription's due date without the customer taking any action. Manual renewal generates a renewal order and emails the customer a payment link they have to complete themselves. Which one applies depends entirely on the payment gateway used at checkout — not a setting you toggle for an individual subscription.

Why This Happens

WooCommerce Subscriptions doesn't itself process payments — it delegates that to whichever gateway the customer chose at checkout. Gateways that support storing a reusable payment token (Stripe, most direct-integration gateways) enable automatic renewal. Gateways that don't support tokenized recurring charges (standard PayPal Standard, offline methods like bank transfer or cheque) fall back to manual renewal: an order is still generated on schedule, but the customer has to actually complete payment on it.

Side by Side

 Automatic RenewalManual Renewal
Customer action requiredNone — charged automaticallyMust click a payment link and pay
Typical gatewaysStripe, most direct-integration card gatewaysPayPal Standard, bank transfer, cheque
Failed-payment retry system appliesYesNo — there's no charge to retry
Risk of lapsed accessLower — renews unattendedHigher — depends on the customer remembering to pay
Reminder emailsOptional, mostly unnecessaryImportant — WooCommerce sends a renewal-due notice

Common Causes of Confusion

  • Assuming it's a per-subscription setting. It isn't — it's determined by the gateway used at the original checkout, and generally can't be changed on an existing subscription without switching payment method entirely.
  • A subscription unexpectedly on manual renewal. Usually means the original payment method doesn't support tokenized recurring billing, even if the store also accepts other, automatic-capable gateways.
  • Renewal reminder emails not being sent for manual subscriptions. Check WooCommerce → Settings → Emails — the "Customer Renewal Invoice" email specifically covers this and is easy to have accidentally disabled.

What To Do About It

  1. To move a subscription from manual to automatic, the customer needs to update their payment method to one that supports automatic renewal — there's no admin toggle that converts it directly.
  2. To reduce manual-renewal lapses, confirm the Customer Renewal Invoice email is active and consider adding an earlier reminder before the due date, since manual renewal depends entirely on the customer noticing and acting.
  3. To push more customers toward automatic renewal, that's a checkout-design decision — making a tokenized gateway the clear default option reduces the number of subscriptions that end up on manual renewal in the first place.

See custom WooCommerce development if you need the checkout or renewal-reminder flow adjusted to reduce manual-renewal churn.

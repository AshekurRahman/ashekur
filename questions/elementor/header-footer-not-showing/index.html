Why Isn't My Elementor Custom Header or Footer Showing?

SHORT ANSWER

A Theme Builder header or footer template not appearing is most often caused by its display conditions not actually matching the page being viewed, the template being saved as a draft rather than published, or the active theme applying its own header/footer output in a way that conflicts with Elementor's, particularly with a theme not built with Elementor Theme Builder compatibility in mind.

ON THIS PAGE

  1. Why This Happens
  2. Common Causes
  3. How to Fix It

Why This Happens

A Theme Builder template only renders where its display conditions say it should — if those conditions were set incorrectly, or the template still sits as a draft, it simply won't appear anywhere despite being fully designed. Some themes also render their own header/footer markup independently of Elementor, requiring theme-specific settings or a genuinely Elementor-compatible theme for the Theme Builder template to take over properly.

Common Causes

  • Display conditions not matching the page, the most common cause — a condition set to a specific category when it should be "Entire Site," for instance.
  • The template left as a draft rather than published, since a draft template never renders on the live site regardless of its display conditions.
  • Conflicting display conditions from multiple templates, where a more specific but unintended condition takes priority over the one actually wanted.
  • The active theme rendering its own header/footer independently, particularly with a non-Elementor-optimized theme that doesn't hand off header/footer rendering properly.
  • Caching serving a stale version of the page from before the template was published or its conditions updated.

How to Fix It

  1. Confirm the template is published, not left as a draft.
  2. Review and correct its display conditions to match the intended pages.
  3. Check for conflicting templates with overlapping conditions and adjust priority as needed.
  4. Confirm theme compatibility, switching to a genuinely Elementor-friendly theme if the current one is fighting with Theme Builder output.
  5. Clear all caching after making changes.
